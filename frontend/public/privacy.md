# Privacy Policy — PPS Connect

**Publisher**: Prathvi Power Solutions  
**Contact**: harivanshraj9@gmail.com  
**Last updated**: 5 February 2026

PPS Connect ("the app") is a private business tool that gives Prathvi Power
Solutions team members access to internal expense, HRMS, billing, and DISCOM
consumer records. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

## 1. Data we collect

The app collects only what is needed to run the business features you use:

- **Account details** you enter — name, email, mobile, employee ID, department, designation.
- **Business data** you or your colleagues create inside the app — expenses, salary sheets, invoices, payment history, consumer lookups.
- **Login events** — timestamp, IP address, device type — recorded in our internal audit log for security.
- **Bill photos** you optionally attach to an expense — stored on our server as part of the expense record.

The app does **not** collect location, contacts, calendar, SMS, health data or advertising identifiers.

## 2. Where data is stored

All data is stored on servers managed by Prathvi Power Solutions. The app
communicates only with `prathvipowersolutions.com` (and its preview / staging
subdomains) over HTTPS.

## 3. Who can see the data

Only authenticated Prathvi Power Solutions employees whose accounts have been
granted access by a system administrator. Roles range from Super Admin (full)
to Viewer (read-only).

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

## 4. Permissions the app requests

- **Internet + Network state** — required to load the app.
- **Camera / Photos** (optional) — only when you tap "Snap Bill" to attach a
  photo of a paper receipt to an expense. The camera is off at all other times.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

The app stores your authentication token in the device's private storage so
that you stay signed in. Sessions expire automatically after 12 hours of
inactivity. You can sign out at any time from the top-right user menu.

## 6. Children

PPS Connect is a business tool for employees and is not directed to children
under 13.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask an administrator to:
- Export a copy of your data
- Correct any inaccurate personal information
- Deactivate or delete your account

## 8. Changes

If we materially change how the app handles data, we will update this page
and record the change under "Last updated" above.
